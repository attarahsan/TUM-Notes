Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · TUM Notes Hub by Suhrani Developers

1. What we collect

When you use TUM Notes Hub we collect only what the service needs:

  • Account info — your name and email address from Google sign-in.
  • Content you upload — study notes (PDFs, documents, images) and cover pictures you choose to sell or share.
  • Payment proofs — screenshots you voluntarily upload to verify a purchase, subscription or withdrawal.
  • Transaction records — orders, wallet balance, earnings and withdrawal requests tied to your account.

2. How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: listings, purchases, seller payouts and withdrawals.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud or copyright abuse.
  • To contact you about your orders or account if needed.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Google sign-in

We use Google OAuth only to identify you (name and email). We never see or store your Google password.

4. Data storage

Data is stored securely with our cloud provider (Supabase). Uploaded files are kept in private storage buckets and shared only as needed for the service (for example, a purchased note shown to its buyer, or a payment screenshot shown to the admin for verification).

5. Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete your account data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Deleting your account removes your profile; transaction records required for accounting may be retained.

6. Children

TUM Notes Hub is intended for university students. If you are under 16, please use it with a parent or guardian's permission.
